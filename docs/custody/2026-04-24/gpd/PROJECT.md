# ZPE-Digital-Ink

## What This Is

ZPE-Digital-Ink is a proof-first digital-ink utility-kernel program built around the `.zpink` packet contract and the `zpe_ink` codec. The program is no longer allowed to optimize for a good-looking local story; the active job is to turn the Phase 1 proof bundle into a contradiction-free, license-correct, same-corpus authority surface and then decide whether a real commercial wedge survives.

Phase 1 proved a credible local transport lane on the Mac M1 Air. It did not prove broad hard-corpus authority, primitive-token runtime identity, or enterprise readiness. The next work therefore starts from contradiction resolution, comparator freeze, and hard-corpus honesty rather than further narrative expansion.

## Core Research Question

Can ZPE-Digital-Ink become a commercially disruptive, drop-in ink utility kernel under a contradiction-free and same-corpus-honest evidence surface, or must the claim stay narrowly scoped to structured-tier transport plus explicit downstream candidate branches?
