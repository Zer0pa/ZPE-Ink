# Roadmap: ZPE-Digital-Ink

## Overview

This roadmap treats ZPE-Digital-Ink as a contradiction-first engineering program. Phase 1 established the local transport lane. The next job is not more story, adapter breadth, or wedge inflation; it is to resolve the PASS-versus-NO-GO surface, freeze comparators, rerun hard corpora honestly, and only then decide whether a broader commercial wedge survives.
