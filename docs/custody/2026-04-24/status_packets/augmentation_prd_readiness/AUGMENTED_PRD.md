## Augmentation Research Synthesis

### Shared conclusion

All three research packets converge on a narrow reopening, not a broad restart:

- `Claude` argues for a trajectory-foundation-model repositioning: integer-canvas ink tokens plus a UniTraj-style pretraining path and an InkSight-adjacent interchange standard.
- `Gemini` argues for a stricter interoperability thesis: preserve spatiotemporal motor-intent structure and make multi-runtime equivalence more mathematically explicit.
- `Perplexity` argues for a binary/discrete codebook layer to make runtime-agnostic interchange architecturally grounded rather than merely empirically tested.

### Synthesis adopted for ZPE-Ink

- Preserve the near-term lane as interoperability truth reconciliation and local rerun closure.
- Treat augmentation as conditional on that closure, not as a substitute for it.
- Use the augmentation research to define the *next* bounded engineering slice once the lane is legitimately reopened:
  - integer-canvas token surface
  - or binary/discrete codebook surface
  - or a hybrid path that keeps `.zpink` as the transport truth and adds a candidate token layer alongside it

### Research claims explicitly rejected for this wave

- Any claim that ZPE-Ink is already a modern InkML successor.
- Any claim that the lane should reopen around broad handwriting-product or enterprise-readiness language.
- Any requirement to jump immediately to GPU or RunPod.
- Any attempt to reframe the archived `NO-GO` as obsolete without a new evidence-backed decision packet.
